# Document Wording

Use selected ASD-STE100 principles as a STE-inspired default for authored English
document prose. This policy does not claim full ASD-STE100 compliance. Full
compliance requires the applicable writing rules and controlled dictionary.

Apply the policy before finalization, validation, revision freeze, content
hashing, or an approval request. The current request and task constraints take
precedence. Preserve the requested reader language; this policy does not
authorize translation. Do not persist inferred preferences or change a reader
profile.

- Use a clear subject and a direct action when the actor is known.
- Prefer one main idea per sentence. Keep logical connections explicit.
- Put a prerequisite condition before the action when the reader needs it first.
- Use a consistent term for the same concept.
- Prefer familiar words when they preserve technical precision.
- Retain explanations, causal relationships, qualifications, and required detail.
- Preserve the force of `must`, `should`, and `may`. Preserve negation,
  conditions, exceptions, scope, uncertainty, attribution, and numeric thresholds.

Keep the existing headings, section order and boundaries, list hierarchy,
table shape, diagrams, and required template fields. A wording-only request
does not authorize view/depth selection or hierarchy changes. Separately
requested structural adaptation retains its existing gates. Do not force
explanations into commands or impose sentence-length quotas.

Preserve commands, code, paths, identifiers, URLs, link targets, schema keys,
exact quotations, native patch blocks, and evidence values byte for byte.
Check the revised prose against its facts, requirements, constraints, and
uncertainty; repair any change of meaning before finalization.

Existing supplied artifacts remain unchanged unless the user requests revision.
Registration, import, copying, archiving, and centralization are not rewriting
operations. Edits after approval follow the existing stale-decision rules.
Reading this reference does not activate delivery, structural adaptation,
routing, preference persistence, or a human gate.

Sources: [ASD FAQ](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html) and
[ASD guidance](https://asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html).
