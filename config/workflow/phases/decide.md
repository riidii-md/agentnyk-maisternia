# /work-decide - Record an Explicit Human Decision

Routing gate (lazy): load `work-routing` only when `$ARGUMENTS` has a plausible explicit route, an active session route exists, or the exact `.maisternia/work-routing.json` or `${XDG_CONFIG_HOME:-~/.config}/maisternia/work-routing.json` exists. Otherwise continue locally without loading it. After loading, continue only with its cleaned task.

Record one explicit human decision without relying on chat implication. Use
direction mode to choose among materially different approaches before planning.
Use plan mode after the explicit AI review choice to approve, request changes, or reject
the exact plan revision. A recommendation generated by an agent is not a decision.

Input:

`$ARGUMENTS`

Accepted forms:

```text
/work-decide direction <options, recommendation, and human response>
/work-decide direction <reviewed direction path and mdmaid.desk plan-decision result JSON>
/work-decide direction <direction path, explicit AI review skip, and human response>
/work-decide plan <reviewed plan path and human response>
/work-decide plan <reviewed plan path and mdmaid.desk review result JSON>
/work-decide plan <plan path, explicit AI review skip, and human response>
```

Infer the mode only when the artifact and response make it unambiguous.
When the architectural direction gate applied, an option choice in conversation
is not gate approval. Require either a passing direction review or an explicit
AI review skip, followed by an explicit human decision for the exact artifact
revision and content hash.

Return:

- Decision title
- Human decision and rationale
- mdmaid.desk review request ID, outcome, and human response text when supplied by
  mdmaid.desk
- Direction or plan artifact path and content hash when applicable
- Passing AI review evidence or the explicit AI review skip
- Accepted constraints, assumptions, and risks
- Requested changes or rejection reason
- Decision author and time when available
- Next phase appropriate to the mode

Direction and plan approval are valid only for the recorded content hash.
Material direction changes invalidate the direction decision and return to
direction plus its AI review choice; material plan changes invalidate the plan
decision and return to planning plus its AI review choice. Do not let an agent
approve its own direction or plan, infer approval from silence, or treat
document registration, opening, or closing as a human decision.

In particular, never let an agent approve its own plan.

For an authenticated mdmaid.desk `plan-decision` result, verify that its
document ID, revision, and semantic decision mode identify the delivered
direction or plan and that the local artifact still matches the recorded
content hash. Map `approved` to approval, `changes_requested` to the matching
direction or planning phase and its explicit AI review choice, `rejected` to
stop or reshape, and `stale` to no decision and fresh publication. Preserve the
human response text verbatim in the decision record for every outcome; never
replace it with a button label or agent summary.
